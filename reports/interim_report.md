# Ethiopia Financial Inclusion Analysis – Interim Report

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes financial inclusion trends in Ethiopia using a unified dataset containing observations, events, targets, and impact links. The objective is to understand historical trends, identify important drivers of financial inclusion, and prepare the dataset for later forecasting and impact modeling tasks.

---

## 2. Data Enrichment Summary

The dataset was enriched with additional information related to digital financial services and financial inclusion initiatives.

The enrichment process included:

* Addition of a Fayda Digital ID Expansion event
* Addition of a Telebirr user observation
* Addition of an impact relationship linking digital identity expansion to account ownership

All additions were documented in the accompanying data_enrichment_log.md file.

---

## 3. Key Insights

### Insight 1

Account ownership has increased substantially over the past decade, demonstrating significant progress in financial inclusion.

### Insight 2

Growth in account ownership slowed between 2021 and 2024 compared with earlier periods.

### Insight 3

Digital financial services such as Telebirr appear to be important contributors to increased financial access and usage.

### Insight 4

The dataset combines observations, events, targets, and impact relationships, enabling future analysis of how policy and market events influence financial inclusion outcomes.

### Insight 5

Confidence levels vary across records, indicating differences in evidence quality and certainty.

---

## 4. Preliminary Event–Indicator Observations

Several events in the dataset appear to be associated with improvements in financial inclusion indicators.

Examples include:

* Telebirr launch and increased digital financial service usage.
* Safaricom market entry and telecommunications infrastructure expansion.
* Digital identity initiatives and potential increases in account ownership.

These relationships are represented in the impact links included in the dataset.

---

## 5. Data Limitations

The analysis identified several limitations:

* Small dataset size.
* Missing values in several fields.
* Uneven temporal coverage across indicators.
* Limited observations for some indicators.
* Impact estimates are partially based on assumptions and external evidence rather than direct measurement.

---

## 6. Conclusion

The exploratory analysis demonstrates substantial progress in Ethiopia's financial inclusion landscape. Digital financial services, telecommunications expansion, and digital identity initiatives appear to be important drivers of inclusion. The dataset is suitable for the next phase of the project, including event impact modeling and forecasting.
